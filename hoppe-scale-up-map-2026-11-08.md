Companion to the universe map (same date). One build, run at
every tier: points in relationship, a completion seat, a
restart where completions become the next tier's points.
The flower seats each tier (center + rings; completions at
the knots). Every tier is a full circuit — everything
reused, no leg ends in the void.

THE READING RULE [HIS]: a reading is an arrival landing at
this end, now, in order — like a thrown rock that took a
long time to get here. Time is read on the path; place on
the address (X, Y, Z, K). Distance is the count of stops
between seats, not a substance. [Context: a light-year is
the span light crosses in one year ≈ 5.88 trillion miles;
Andromeda ≈ 2.5 million of those. We can see it because it
is enormous and bright — a trillion stars' worth of arrivals
still landing — not because the span is small.]

## Tier 0 — The plasma [HIS start direction; content CHECKED]
Quark-gluon plasma: quarks and gluons, nothing bound yet.
Six quark flavors present (up, down, charm, strange, top,
bottom) at this heat; only up and down survive the cooling
into lasting matter. The relationship at this tier is the
gluon exchange itself — the tie before there are things to
tie. Binding-share ladder's root lives here: inside what
this tier builds, the relationship is ~99% of the mass.

## Tier 1 — Proton and neutron [HIS; charges CHECKED]
Quarks tie in threes. Proton: 2 up + 1 down = +1
(2/3 + 2/3 − 1/3), the matter seat. Neutron: 2 down + 1 up
= 0, the transition seat — the one that flips. Electron:
the worker, the pair partner. The creation point is the
seat the pair comes out of — neither member. Quarks stay
represented inside (they never appear alone).

## Tier 2 — The nucleus [HIS seats; bindings CHECKED]
Protons and neutrons in relationship; the relationship's
share drops to ~1%. The first completed cell appears
already at this tier: helium-4 — two protons, two neutrons —
the square, 28.30 MeV held, the tightest small package.
The alpha ladder climbs from here (+4: 12, 16, 20 …) to the
iron turn, where producing stops and every road out costs.

## Tier 3 — The atom [HIS; shell counts CHECKED]
Nucleus as the point; electrons in shells around it. Shell
capacities are a 2-DEGREE count (the pair only): 2, 8, 18,
32 (= 2n²) — against the flower's 6-degree count (3 strips
× 2): a count-basis difference, not a different law.
Completions (closed shells: He, Ne, Ar…) are the seats
where energy takes spatial distance and propagates —
chemical hops run electron to electron, shell to shell,
atom to atom: the hop at this tier.

## Tier 4 — The molecule [HIS grammar]
Elements become points. Generation grammar runs verbatim:
√H × √H + O = water; √O × √O + C = carbon dioxide — parts
in relationship at their proportion, and a new thing as
its own self. Components stay represented (hydrogen in
water still exists, and comes back out whole). The
relationship's share at this tier: ~a billionth.

## Tier 5 — Bodies and materials [HIS map]
Molecules build cells; cells build bodies. Body circuit
(his seats): heart the center, lungs the flow, blood the
smart filters, lymph the return/recycle, skin the outer
boundary. Water passes through as the working fluid and
returns loaded; oxygen comes in and is used because the
return leg is built in (plants run the other half). States
stay literal: solid, liquid, gas, plasma are seats, not
labels.

## Tier 6 — Planet and star [HIS synthesis]
The sun loop: hydrogen and helium build from each other;
inside the sun, subatomic makes atomic (Tiers 0–2 running
inside a star); contained heat builds pressure until it
releases as sunlight, propagating outward as energy in
2 forms (unnamed). Earth receives: carbon brings life,
oxygen sustains it. Sun gaseous, Earth matter — the states
seated at system scale. Loops branch; every branch closes.

## Tier 7 — Galaxies and the web [HIS]
States extended: the mass is the proton's seat; the stars
are the electron's (energy, hopping); the black hole is the
neutron's (transition — and the recycle intake: what goes
in comes back, nothing built right is discarded). Galaxies:
center seat, hop motion at the edges read with a pull
ruler is the old mismeasurement. The web: strands
(filaments), knots (nodes), and holes whose centers are
seats — the same flower, wrapped: ring, knot, center.

## The tie-back [HIS]
Same grammar at every tier: a seed that is a pair; stages
in relationship; completions at knots; representation with
nothing disappearing; a restart made of finished things;
propagation born at completion seats; a return leg on
every output. The flower is not a picture OF the pattern —
